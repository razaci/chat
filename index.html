Different places.
Same conversation.

This HTML launcher opens your online chat app in your browser.

Open chat ↗
  1. Enter your display name.
  2. Create a conversation and copy the room link.
  3. Open the same room link on your other device.

Internet access is required. The hosted app is currently private to your ChatGPT account; sign in with that account on each device. Other people need site access before they can join. Messages are stored on the server and are not end-to-end encrypted.
